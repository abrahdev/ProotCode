    Turn on Wireless debugging

    Open Settings → Developer options and enable Wireless debugging. It requires Android 11 or later. If you don’t see Developer options, tap Build number seven times in About phone.

    Pair this device

    On the Wireless debugging screen, tap Pair device with pairing code, then enter what it shows.

    Connect

    Enter the IP address and port shown at the top of the Wireless debugging screen. The port is different from the pairing one.

    ADB connected
